This is the only prompt structure that works. Everything else is noise. Here is the problem: you are writing prompts like emails, and the model reads them like a checklist. Last week I ran the same task twenty times with three structures. Only one of them finished every time. So: put the goal first, then the constraints, then the one example. Not three examples, one. Do that and you stop babysitting the output. If you want the template, it is in the caption.
